How to Buy Steel Hollow Sections: A Practical Checklist

A reliable purchase starts with a clear specification and delivery brief. Use this guide to prepare the information a supplier needs before you compare prices or confirm an order.

◷ 8 MIN READ•Reviewed: November 2026
TECHNICALMEPSELECT / SPECIFY / SOURCE
KEY TAKEAWAY

A precise enquiry starts with a precise specification.

01

1. Define the application and service conditions

Identify whether the section is intended for frames, fabricated components, equipment, industrial assemblies, building work or an architectural application. Review installation space, orientation, connection method, environment and loading with the project team. For load-bearing or safety-critical use, the final section and wall thickness must follow engineering calculations and the project requirements; this guide is not a substitute for structural design.

02

2. Choose the correct product family

A square hollow section has equal outside dimensions; a rectangular hollow section has two different outside dimensions. Steel tubes and French-style frames are separate product groups and should not be compared only by name or appearance. Select the family from the drawing, installation space and connection details, then review the available catalogue sizes.

03

3. State dimensions, wall thickness and length clearly

For a square section, state outside width × outside height × nominal wall thickness in millimetres, for example 40 × 40 × 2. For a rectangular section, state width and height in a consistent order. For a tube, state outside diameter and wall thickness. Add required stock length, cut length and any project-specific tolerance. Do not confuse outside dimensions with internal dimensions.

04

4. Confirm standards, grade and quality documents

If the drawing or contract specifies a manufacturing standard, steel grade, mechanical properties, dimensional tolerances, surface condition or test certificate, include the exact requirement in your enquiry. Grade or compliance cannot be inferred from appearance, colour or a product name. Ask the supplier to confirm the applicable product specification and available documentation before ordering.

05

5. Define quantity and delivery details

State the quantity as number of lengths, approximate weight or number of fabricated pieces. Clarify whether standard stock lengths or custom cuts are required. List each size and its quantity separately. Include destination city, unloading constraints, packaging, target delivery date and whether transport is included or arranged separately.

06

6. Compare quotations on the same basis

Send each supplier the same product family, dimensions, thickness, grade or standard (if required), length, quantity, destination and delivery timing. Compare more than the headline price: record the quotation date and validity, availability or lead time, payment terms, technical documents and transport cost. Website prices are indicative; confirm the final price and supply terms directly with the Tehran office.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a steel profile quotation?

Provide the section type, outside dimensions, nominal wall thickness, quantity, required length, delivery destination and any required standard or technical document.

Can wall thickness be selected from dimensions alone?

No. Application, loading, connections, support conditions and design requirements matter. For structural use, the project engineer must approve the selection.

Are website prices final?

No. Confirm the price, availability, lead time and delivery terms for the exact order with the Tehran office.

How should I list an order with several sizes?

Use a separate line for each size, thickness and quantity to reduce ambiguity in the quotation.
