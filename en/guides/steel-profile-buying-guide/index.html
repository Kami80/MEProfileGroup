How to Buy Steel Hollow Sections: A Practical Checklist

A good steel profile enquiry starts with clear specifications, not price alone. Define the application, section dimensions, nominal wall thickness, quantity and delivery destination before comparing quotations.

A good steel profile enquiry starts with clear specifications, not price alone. Define the application, section dimensions, nominal wall thickness, quantity and delivery destination before comparing quotations.

1. Define the application

Start by identifying how the section will be used: frames, fabricated components, equipment, industrial assemblies or building work. Consider the installation space, connection method and project requirements. For load-bearing applications, the final section and thickness must be selected with the project engineer and relevant design requirements.

2. Choose the section shape

A square hollow section has equal outside width and height. A rectangular hollow section has different outside dimensions. The right choice depends on the design, available space, orientation and fabrication needs. Explore square hollow sections and rectangular hollow sections.

3. State dimensions and wall thickness clearly

List outside dimensions and nominal wall thickness separately, for example 40 × 40 mm with a nominal 2 mm wall. Include the unit of measurement and any project-specific tolerances. The 40 × 40 × 2 mm specification page shows how a listed size is presented.

4. Confirm standards and technical documents

If a project requires a steel grade, manufacturing standard, mechanical properties, dimensional tolerances or test documents, state those requirements before ordering and ask the supplier to confirm them. Do not infer a grade or standard from appearance alone.

5. Specify quantity, length and destination

State the number of lengths or approximate weight, required length, packaging needs, delivery city and target date. If the enquiry includes multiple sizes, list the quantity for each line separately.

6. Send a complete quotation request

Include product type, dimensions, thickness, quantity, destination and any technical requirements. Website prices are for initial reference; confirm the current price and supply terms directly with the Tehran office. Use the quotation request form to send your details.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a steel profile quotation?

Specify the section type, outside dimensions, nominal wall thickness, quantity, required length and delivery destination. Add any required standard or technical documents.

Is wall thickness selected by dimensions alone?

No. Application, loading, connections and design requirements also matter. For structural use, consult the project engineer.

Are website prices final order prices?

No. Confirm the price, availability and delivery terms for each order with the Tehran office.
