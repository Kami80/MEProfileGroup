What Affects Steel Profile and Tube Prices?

A steel product quotation is not determined by one variable. Dimensions, nominal wall thickness, technical specifications, order volume and delivery arrangements can all affect the final offer.

A steel product quotation is not determined by one variable. Dimensions, nominal wall thickness, technical specifications, order volume and delivery arrangements can all affect the final offer.

1. Product type and section dimensions

Steel tubes, square hollow sections, rectangular hollow sections and French-style frames are different product groups. Within each group, dimensions and wall thickness vary. Compare like-for-like products with matching specifications.

2. Nominal wall thickness

Wall thickness is a key product specification and contributes to the weight of a length and the amount of material used. Check that quotations use the same nominal thickness and measurement units.

3. Steel specification and technical requirements

If a project requires a particular grade, standard, tolerance, surface condition or quality document, state it in the enquiry. Ask the supplier to confirm what can be provided; do not assume compliance without documentation.

4. Order quantity and required length

Order volume, the mix of sizes, number of lengths and required cut length may affect supply planning and related costs. List the quantity for each size when requesting multiple products.

5. Delivery destination and transport

The destination, loading arrangements, transport type and delivery timing are relevant to a final quotation. Export enquiries also require destination and commercial requirements to be reviewed separately.

6. Timing and quotation validity

Steel-market conditions can change. Ask for the quotation date and validity period. Website prices are for initial reference; current prices and final supply terms are updated and confirmed directly by the Tehran office.

How to compare quotations fairly

Send every supplier the same details: product type, dimensions, thickness, required standard or grade, quantity, length and destination. Compare price together with delivery timing, payment terms and technical documentation. You can use the quotation form to prepare your request.

Frequently asked questions

Why can a price change over time?

Market conditions, raw material costs, production and transport costs and quotation validity can change. Confirm the current offer with the Tehran office.

Can I compare suppliers by size alone?

For a fair comparison, also match wall thickness, grade or standard, length, order quantity, delivery terms and technical documents.

How are online prices confirmed?

Website prices are for initial reference. Confirm final price, availability and delivery terms directly with the Tehran office.
