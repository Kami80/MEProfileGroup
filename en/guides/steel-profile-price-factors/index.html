What Affects Steel Profile and Tube Prices?

A steel quotation is not determined by the product name alone. To receive and compare meaningful offers, align the technical specification, order quantity and delivery terms, then check how long the price remains valid.

◷ 7 MIN READ•Reviewed: November 2026
TECHNICAL₮SELECT / SPECIFY / SOURCE
KEY TAKEAWAY

For a fair price comparison, align specifications and delivery terms.

01

1. Product type and section dimensions

Steel tubes, square hollow sections, rectangular hollow sections and French-style frames are different product families. Each family includes different dimensions and wall thicknesses. Compare like-for-like items: a quotation for a different section or thickness is not a direct price comparison.

02

2. Wall thickness and order weight

Nominal wall thickness is a core product specification and affects material usage and the weight of a length. When comparing offers, match the nominal thickness, measurement unit and stock length. If a price is quoted by weight, ask for the weight basis and calculation method.

03

3. Steel grade, standards and technical requirements

If the project requires a particular grade, manufacturing standard, mechanical properties, tolerances, surface condition or quality documents, include them in the enquiry. These requirements can change the range of acceptable products and the supply conditions. Do not assume compliance without confirmation or documentation.

04

4. Order quantity, size mix and length

Number of lengths, total weight, the mix of sizes and any custom cutting can affect supply planning and preparation. For multi-size orders, list the quantity for each item separately. Mention special packaging, cutting or handling requirements before requesting a quotation.

05

5. Destination, loading and transport

Destination, loading access, vehicle type, unloading and delivery timing can affect the final delivered cost. Export orders may require separate review of destination, documents, trade terms and transport requirements. Clarify whether a quotation includes delivery or is for collection at the supplier location.

06

6. Quotation date and validity

Market and supply conditions can change. Record the quotation date, validity period, preparation lead time and delivery date. Website prices are for initial reference; the current price, availability and final order terms are confirmed directly by the Tehran office.

07

7. How to compare quotations fairly

Send every supplier the same product type, dimensions, thickness, grade or standard, length, quantity, destination and delivery timing. Compare unit price and pricing basis, stated taxes or additional charges, transport, lead time, payment terms, technical documents and validity. Ask for written clarification where any item is missing.

Frequently asked questions

Why can the price of the same size change over time?

Market conditions, raw material and production costs, transport, availability and quotation validity can change. Confirm the current offer for your order with the Tehran office.

Is size alone enough to compare prices?

No. Match wall thickness, grade or standard, length, quantity, pricing basis and delivery terms as well.

How are online prices confirmed?

Website prices are for initial reference. Confirm the final price, availability and delivery conditions for the specific order with the Tehran office.

What should I send to several suppliers?

Send the same product type, dimensions, thickness, required standard, quantity, length, destination and delivery date to each supplier.
