Square vs Rectangular Hollow Sections: How to Choose

Square and rectangular hollow sections are both hollow steel profiles, but their outside dimensions differ. Selection should follow the design, installation space, loading direction and fabrication requirements—not appearance or price alone.

The geometric difference

A square hollow section has equal outside width and height. A rectangular hollow section has different outside width and height. Both forms can be supplied in different nominal wall thicknesses; confirm the exact product specification before ordering.

When to consider a square section

A square section may suit designs that call for equal outside dimensions, including certain frames, supports and fabricated assemblies. Confirm the dimensions and thickness against the drawing and actual use. See the square hollow section range.

When to consider a rectangular section

A rectangular section may suit designs that require different outside dimensions or have a specific space constraint. Check the width-to-height ratio and orientation against the drawing and connection method. See rectangular hollow sections.

Do not compare shape alone

Wall thickness, steel specification, tolerances, length, cutting and connection methods and the service environment can all matter. Structural performance cannot be determined simply by whether a section is square or rectangular; use the project calculations and applicable standards.

What to include in your enquiry

Send the drawing or dimensions, nominal wall thickness, quantity, required length and delivery destination. For load-bearing use, include the relevant design requirements from the project engineer.

Frequently asked questions

Is a square section always stronger than a rectangular one?

No. Performance depends on dimensions, thickness, material properties, loading direction, connections and support conditions. Compare using engineering calculations.

What dimensions should I provide?

For both shapes, provide outside dimensions and wall thickness. For a rectangular section, state width and height separately and use consistent units.

Are all listed sizes in stock?

The catalogue shows selected sizes. Availability and supply conditions must be confirmed for each enquiry.
