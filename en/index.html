Our story

A new name.
Generations of expertise.

Pouya Middle East Profile Holding was formally established in the Iranian year 1404 through the collaboration of Parsin Tisfon Ilya and Kara Felez Sarmad. Its founders brought more than a decade of independent experience in Iran’s steel market, built on the knowledge and work of earlier generations.

Today, the group draws on the capacity of four factories across different cities and the Parsin Felez central warehouse. This combined network is supported by more than 50 years of accumulated industry heritage, and is focused on reliable quality and competitive supply.

Common questions

What would you like to know?

Answers to common questions about our products, supply network and ordering process.

What steel products do you manufacture and supply?

Our range includes industrial steel tubes, square and rectangular hollow sections and French-style frames, with specifications matched to each enquiry.

Which sizes do you list?

Our product catalogue covers the dimensions and wall thicknesses in each category. Select a specification to request a quotation.

Do you deliver across Iran?

Yes. Nationwide supply and shipping within Iran form part of our activities. Transportation details depend on the delivery destination and order volume.

Do you handle international export inquiries?

Yes. The holding welcomes export requests, subject to destination, product specifications and applicable commercial requirements.

How many factories are in your production network?

The group works with the capacity of four factories in different cities, along with the Parsin Felez central warehouse.

When was the holding founded?

The holding formally began operations in 1404 in the Solar Hijri calendar. Its founders previously had more than 10 years of independent steel-market experience, supported by over 50 years of intergenerational industry heritage.
