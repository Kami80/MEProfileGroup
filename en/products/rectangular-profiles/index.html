Frequently asked questions

Which dimensions are available to enquire about?

The company catalogue includes rectangular dimensions ranging from 25 × 10 to 100 × 40 mm, with individual wall thicknesses listed on this page.

Is 60 × 30 available in several thicknesses?

The 60 × 30 mm section is listed with nominal wall thicknesses of 1.6, 2 and 2.5 mm. Specify the required thickness in your enquiry.

How do I request a rectangular profile quotation?

Specify both outside dimensions, wall thickness, approximate quantity, preferred lengths, destination and fabrication requirements.
